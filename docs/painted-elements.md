# 草地和沙滩的彩绘元素

两张透明图集由内置 ImageGen 生成。原版小猫与放风筝男孩的 PNG 作为风格参照：

- `prototype/assets/meadow-painted-elements.png`
- `prototype/assets/beach-painted-elements.png`

场景从透明图集裁取角色与物件，在原地形场中安排位置，并计算扇翅、跳跃、花枝和船帆的动画姿态。

## 共用提示词

Use the two attached existing transparent PNGs ONLY as style references. Create an original painted game asset sheet matching their children's book gouache and coloured pencil texture, softly irregular pigment edges, controlled brown ink line weight, matte block-like shadows and bright lively highlights across readable hand-painted silhouettes. Strong dry paper tooth in all fills. Avoid smooth vector or 3D rendering. Match their three-quarter slightly aerial storybook views, with consistent illumination from upper left. Do not depict the references themselves. Output an image with actual transparent background and exactly six separate assets in a regular 3 columns by 2 rows grid, clear alpha gutters, no shadows beyond the object, no words, no marks or grids. Each subject must occupy the middle 70% of its own cell and be completely detached from others. Readability at 25–70px desktop scale.

Top row left to right: lush loose cluster of 6–8 coral, white and warm yellow wildflowers with rounded botanical leaves, group of 3 russet toadstools with speckling and ochre gills, a single orange monarch-like butterfly viewed from directly overhead, head up, wings fully open, bold maroon margins. Bottom row left to right: single small firefly seen from above with brown body, glowing golden abdomen and two painted delicate wings, an expressive cream meadow rabbit in side-three-quarter view facing right, ears high, front paws touching ground, a compact rustic stone and timber windmill tower with warm terracotta roof, timber arched doorway and small blue window, NO blades on the mill because blades animate as a separate asset. The applied matte paper and gouache brush texture should be strong and visible. Asset proportions suited to the referenced large-eyed stylized characters.
